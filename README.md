# PERFECT Atlas

The map of what [PERFECT](https://www.youtube.com/@perfect-quant) teaches, across the macrofinancial world:

**P**rogramming · **E**conomics · **R**eview · **F**inance · **E**conometrics · **C**atalog · **T**rading

The atlas starts empty and grows with the channel. Each video adds the ideas it covers: concepts, papers, companies,
historical events, people and tools, linked to one another and to the chapters of
[The Faugeron Lectures on Quantitative Finance](https://github.com/Arthur-Faugeron/PERFECT-Lectures).

Open the atlas: https://arthur-faugeron.github.io/PERFECT-atlas/

Educational content. Not investment advice.
